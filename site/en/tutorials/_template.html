About this tutorial. The screenshots and numbers come from an actual run in November 2026 on macOS with v0.10.0. The model answers differently each time, so the wording and numbers will differ when you follow along; the steps are the same. The screenshots show the Chinese interface; the text uses the labels of the English one (Settings › General › Interface language).

The assistant can be wrong. Its suggestions and answers are for you to confirm; check key numbers and conclusions against the original.
